About

I am a PhD researcher in Computer Science at TU Dortmund University, affiliated with the Research Center Trustworthy Data Science and Security (RC Trust) and the Chair of Human-AI Interaction. Prior to joining TU Dortmund, I spent two years as a researcher at the Human-centric Artificial Intelligence Centre, University of Technology Sydney, investigating EEG-based approaches to dementia detection, emotion recognition, and silent speech decoding. I received an MSc in Electrical Engineering, with a focus on artificial intelligence, from Hongik University, South Korea, and a BSc in Computer Science from Can Tho University of Technology, Vietnam.

My work centers on brain-computer interfaces and gaze-based interaction, and on how these technologies can deepen our understanding of humans, support personal growth, and meaningfully improve people's lives.
